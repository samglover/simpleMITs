What are MITs?

MITs are the two or three most important things you could do each day. Even if you do nothing else, you'll have accomplished some important things!

Setting your MITs is a daily practice. Every morning, review your lists, calendars, inboxes, and project boards, and write down two or three tasks to be your focus for the day. It's okay to have four or five MITs, but if you have six or seven (or more), maybe they all aren't the most important things you could be doing. Keeping your MITs manageable helps you be more productive.

No matter what else you do today, get your MITs done.

I first learned about MITs from this post on Zen Habits, which was inspired by a post on Lifehacker that's no longer available.

What is simpleMITs?

I wanted a simple way to keep track of my MITs on my phone or computer, but separate from my regular task lists, project management software, email inboxes, and calendars.

By design, simpleMITs is, well, simple. What you see is what you get. You don't even need to log in; all your tasks are stored in your browser's local storage. But you can log in if you want to sync your tasks between devices.

simpleMITs works like an app, too! Just add it to the home screen of your phone or tablet.

Tasks:

  • To complete a task, click or tap the number next to it.
  • To delete a task, click or tap the Delete task button next to it.
  • To edit a task, click or tap the text of the task.
  • To reorder tasks, click or tap and drag the Reorder task icon.

Preferences:

  • To make text smaller or larger, click or tap the Decrease text size icon Increase text size icon buttons.
  • To make your task list stretch to fill the screen, click or tap the Maximize task layout icon button. Click or tap the Constrain task layout icon button to return to a fixed-width layout.

Sync:

  • To sync your tasks, click or tap the Sync icon button. If you are signed in it will have a green dot: Sync icon with green dot.
  • While you are signed in, your tasks will be synced with all your signed-in browsers via the cloud. You can still use simpleMITs if you are offline.

For updates, see the changelog.

Where are my tasks stored, who has access to them, and how secure are they?

With sync off (the default), your tasks are only stored in your browser's local storage on your computer or device. If you switch browsers, log in as a different user, or use a different computer or device, you won't have access to them. Clearing the browser cache will also clear your tasks and preferences.

With sync on, your data (email address, tasks, and a timestamp) is synced with a database hosted by Supabase. Because Supabase controls the database server, its staff can access your data, so you should review its privacy policy.

The database admin can also access your data. If you are using simpleMITs on simplemits.com, that's me. I will not access your data, but I may need to delete it (if the database becomes too large, for example).

If you aren't comfortable with that, don't use the sync feature. Or host simpleMITs yourself.
